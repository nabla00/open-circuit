--- layout: post title: Writing a Post in Raw HTML date: 2026-11-15 description: Jekyll also renders plain HTML files inside _posts using the exact same layout. ---

Whenever you want full control over your markup, save your post with an .html extension inside _posts/ and include the same front matter at the top.

Why Use HTML Posts?

  • Custom tables, figures, or semantic elements
  • Direct control over classes and layout
  • Zero conversion needed for existing HTML snippets

Jekyll wraps both .md and .html files in _layouts/post.html automatically.
